How a frame gets built

  1. 01

    Fitting & conversation

    We measure you, not a size chart, and talk through how the bike needs to behave.

  2. 02

    Design & tubing selection

    Geometry is drawn to your numbers; tubing is chosen for your weight and terrain.

  3. 03

    Miter & braze

    Every joint is hand-mitered and brazed in-house, then checked against the original drawing.

  4. 04

    Finish & handoff

    Paint, build kit, and a final fitting session before the frame leaves the shop.
